========================================
SQL - WHERE & OPERATORS
========================================

WHERE:
WHERE is used to filter records according to a condition.

Syntax:
SELECT * FROM table_name
WHERE condition;


Comparison Operators:
=       Equal
>       Greater than
<       Less than
>=      Greater than or equal
<=      Less than or equal
!=      Not equal


Examples:

SELECT * FROM students
WHERE age = 20;

SELECT * FROM students
WHERE age > 20;

SELECT * FROM students
WHERE age < 20;

SELECT * FROM students
WHERE age >= 20;

SELECT * FROM students
WHERE age <= 20;

SELECT * FROM students
WHERE age != 20;


AND:
Both conditions must be TRUE.

Example:
SELECT * FROM students
WHERE age > 20 AND age < 25;


OR:
At least one condition must be TRUE.

Example:
SELECT * FROM students
WHERE age = 20 OR age = 25;


Interview:
Q: What is WHERE?
A: WHERE filters rows based on a condition.

Q: Difference between AND and OR?
A: AND requires all conditions to be TRUE.
   OR requires at least one condition to be TRUE.
